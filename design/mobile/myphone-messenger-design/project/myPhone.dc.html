1myPhone — every screen in dark + light, then tokens. Every phone is live: tap, long-press, type, send.

Accent is a soft mint, used only for my bubbles, the send button and online dots. Everything else is ink on paper. Primary actions (+, Connect) are solid ink so the accent never competes. Font: M PLUS Rounded 1c at 13 / 15 / 20. To test Add device, try media-server, studio-mac or nas; anything else fails.
